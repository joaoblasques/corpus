# Neil Davidson

Co-founder and joint CEO of Red Gate Software (Cambridge, UK; founded 1999, no VC, ~150 employees by 2009; multiple Sunday Times top-100 company awards). Founder of the annual Business of Software conference. Author of *Don't Just Roll the Dice: A Usefully Short Guide to Software Pricing* (2009, CC BY-NC-ND, free). See [/ai-business/sources/dont-just-roll-the-dice.md](/ai-business/sources/dont-just-roll-the-dice.md).
