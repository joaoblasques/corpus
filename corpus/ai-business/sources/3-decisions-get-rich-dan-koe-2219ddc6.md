# These 3 Decisions Will Determine If You Get Rich — Dan Koe

Source: thedankoe.com. Ingested to [/ai-business/creator-economy-philosophy.md].

Three decisions: (1) Aspirational hourly rate ("Set and enforce an aspirational hourly rate" — Naval: $5,000/hr; if it doesn't feel impossible, it's too low; rewires brain to notice higher-leverage opportunities); (2) Concentration of force (getting rich must be your #1 overwhelming desire at least until the actions are ingrained); (3) Forcing functions (use money as a forcing function — move into a more expensive apartment; anxiety drives learning). "None of those decisions involved obsessing over 'what skill to learn.' I had to learn it all."[^1]
