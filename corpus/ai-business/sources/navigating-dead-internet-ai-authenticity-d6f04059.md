# Navigating the Dead Internet Theory: AI's Impact on Authenticity — Arvid Kahl

Source: thebootstrappedfounder.com. Synthesized into [Bootstrapped SaaS Playbook](/ai-business/bootstrapped-saas-playbook.md).

Dead internet theory: the internet is increasingly filled with AI-generated content engaging with other AI-generated content. LinkedIn and Twitter are worst offenders. Machine-to-machine communication for functional purposes (APIs, data pipelines) is fine — the problem is machines *pretending* to be humans to elicit commercial actions. AI-as-means vs. AI-as-end: bad = AI is the output (fully automated cold email blast); good = AI is the input/draft, human completes the work. Design principle: use AI to augment, gate, guard, and guide — not to replace the human act of creative work. Keep humans in the driver's seat.[^1]

This AI-as-means-not-end stance is one perspective in the [Taste as the Moat](/ai-business/taste-as-the-moat.md) synthesis.
