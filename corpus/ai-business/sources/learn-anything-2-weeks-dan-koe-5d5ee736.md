# You Can Learn Anything in 2 Weeks — Dan Koe

Source: thedankoe.com. Synthesized into [Creator Economy Philosophy](/ai-business/creator-economy-philosophy.md).

Technique stacking: skills are composed of techniques; don't try to "learn the guitar" — choose a song to play, find the specific technique needed, try it, solidify it, stack more. Tactical stress as the missing ingredient: put yourself in a high-pressure situation with a strong deadline (quitting your job, moving into an apartment you can't afford). Dan learned After Effects in 2 weeks by committing to video animations he couldn't yet create — because he had to ship videos each week as a survival habit.[^1]
