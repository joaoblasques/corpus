# Why Easy Data Import & Export Boosts Software Retention — Arvid Kahl

Source: thebootstrappedfounder.com. Ingested to [/ai-business/bootstrapped-saas-playbook.md].

Counter-intuitive: making it easy to leave increases likelihood customers will stay. Informed consent is the highest-value retention strategy. Customers lock themselves in through process inertia anyway. Permanent Link case study: publishing a contingency plan on the homepage (if service shuts down, dynamic links convert to static redirects, customers get full Apache/Nginx/Caddy configs to self-host) — "Wouldn't have bought it otherwise." Fathom Analytics built a GA importer when GA changed; import feature became a major acquisition driver; imported historical data creates soft lock-in. Practical: add CSV/tabular export to every table of meaningful business data.[^1]
