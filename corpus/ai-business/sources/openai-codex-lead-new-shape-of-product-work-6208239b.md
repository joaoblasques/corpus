# OpenAI Codex Lead on the New Shape of Product Work — Andrew Ambrosino

Source: Lenny's Newsletter/Podcast, 2026. Show notes; no full transcript available in source.

Andrew Ambrosino leads the Codex desktop app at OpenAI. Key topics: nearly 100% of OpenAI employees use Codex weekly; AI has completely flipped the product development process; "taste" as a professional skill is the most valuable capability in an AI-first workplace; the Codex app would have failed if launched in November rather than February — timing matters; the "zone defense" model for PMs at OpenAI when everyone can build anything; how roles are collapsed on his team (and why eliminating roles entirely is a mistake); vision for a "home base" that coordinates work across ChatGPT, Codex, and existing tools.[^1]
