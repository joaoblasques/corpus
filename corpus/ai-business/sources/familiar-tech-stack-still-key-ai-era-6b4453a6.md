# Why Your Familiar Tech Stack is Still Key in the AI Era — Arvid Kahl

Source: thebootstrappedfounder.com. Ingested to [/ai-business/bootstrapped-saas-playbook.md].

"The best tech stack is the one you already know" still holds in the AI era. A peer suggested it should be "the one the AI knows the most about" — Arvid argues the original stands. Quality of AI-generated code is directly connected to the developer's capacity to review and debug it. If you can't distinguish "okay, yes" from "this doesn't look right," you build a black box. AI builds what people who wrote code in that language thought was best in the past, not what you would build. MCP and tool calling don't change the core argument. Choose a language with a vivid ecosystem (AI amplifies popular languages further); deeply understand one language; don't outsource understanding to AI. "Do not make AI-centric tech choices. Make you-centric tech choices."[^1]
