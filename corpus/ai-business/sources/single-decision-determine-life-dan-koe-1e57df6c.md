# This Single Decision Will Determine Most of Your Life — Dan Koe

Source: thedankoe.com. Synthesized into [Creator Economy Philosophy](/ai-business/creator-economy-philosophy.md).

The outcome of your life is determined by a series of 10–12 bold decisions — train-track shifts. By not making a decision, you let others make decisions for you. The decision of all decisions is rejecting the default path. Wrong decisions are better than no decision — the cost of clarity (bought a $1.1M condo, sold it two months later at a loss: "the cost of clarity"). The more bold decisions made, the more mistakes, the more experience, the more each subsequent mistake costs less.[^1]
