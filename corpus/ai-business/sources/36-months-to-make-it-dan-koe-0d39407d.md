# You Have About 36 Months to Make It — Dan Koe

Source: thedankoe.com. Ingested to [/ai-business/creator-economy-philosophy.md].

The 36-month window is not about "making it" — it's about the definition of "making it" being radically transformed. Three layers of power transfer: internet (from institutions), social media (from employers/publishers), AI (from gatekeepers). Three superpowers now: Learning, Persuasion, Execution. "Silicon solves utility so carbon can transcend to meaning." To prepare: become a philosopher-builder; become a filter for ideas; become an AI orchestrator.[^1]
