# Effortless Marketing for Technical Founders: Leverage SEO — Arvid Kahl

Source: thebootstrappedfounder.com. Ingested to [/ai-business/bootstrapped-saas-playbook.md].

For technical founders who hate marketing: let machines do it. Programmatic SEO: make existing internal data semi-public (podcast pages, transcripts, metadata) to generate thousands of indexed endpoints, hiding most valuable data behind a signup wall. 50% of Podscan's MRR comes from people who Googled for podcast transcripts and found the product. Generative SEO: use unique industry data to surface patterns no one else can see (tourism SaaS: trip cancellation likelihood by country/season). AI-assisted customer scoring: feed customer domains into AI → score signups hours after by in-app activity → reach out personally only to high-score prospects. Documentation as marketing: SEO-friendly knowledge base ranks on Google, answers pre-purchase questions, builds trust.[^1]
