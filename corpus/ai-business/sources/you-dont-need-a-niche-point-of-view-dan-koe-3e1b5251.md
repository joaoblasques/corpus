# You Don't Need a Niche, You Need a Point of View — Dan Koe

Source: thedankoe.com. Ingested to [/ai-business/creator-economy-philosophy.md].

Niche specialization was good advice when the internet was young; specialists are now most likely to be replaced by AI. "Escape competition through authenticity" (Naval). Your point of view can't be replicated — nobody has access to the exact sequence of environmental stimuli that conditioned your mind. James Clear's "1% better every day" is an old idea made new by framing it through his unique mind. AI can regurgitate any idea, but it can't refract it through its non-existent lived philosophy.[^1]
