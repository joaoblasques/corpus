# Why You Should Think Twice Before Starting a Software Business — Arvid Kahl

Source: thebootstrappedfounder.com. Ingested to [/ai-business/bootstrapped-saas-playbook.md].

Seven honest downsides of solo software entrepreneurship: (1) Loneliness — most interactions are with computers; 2am server-down loneliness; (2) Lightning Rod — everything pulls at you with unknown priority; (3) 24/7 Responsibility — always on-call even after hiring; (4) Inertia Mountain — convincing people to change existing solutions takes years; (5) Shifting Sands — customer jobs, regulations, and technology all change constantly; (6) Deprecation Treadmill — Linux LTS ends, MySQL version retired, APIs migrate; (7) Ownership Paradox — must own problems that aren't your fault. The plot twist: all of this is worth it because software businesses have high leverage, high revenue, and strong exit potential. Final advice: start slowly, one hour a day.[^1]
