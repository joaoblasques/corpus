# How to Be More Disagreeable (Create a Reality Distortion Field) — Dan Koe

Source: thedankoe.com. Ingested to [/ai-business/creator-economy-philosophy.md].

Being disagreeable is a sign you know what you want. Four components of a reality distortion field: (1) Obsess over what you want — "Answers are given to those who obsess over the question"; (2) Believe you can create something better — "The world is 99% consumers and 1% creators"; (3) Create a plan that stretches what's possible — impossible timeframes force creative thinking and ruthless prioritization; (4) Master persuasive communication — persuasion vs. manipulation distinction (beneficial to both parties vs. only the manipulator). Steve Jobs as the paradigm case.[^1]
