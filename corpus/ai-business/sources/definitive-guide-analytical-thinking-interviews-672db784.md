# The Definitive Guide to Mastering Analytical Thinking Interviews — Ben Erez

Source: Lenny's Newsletter, by Ben Erez. Ingested to [/ai-business/technical-career.md].

A long-form structured PM interview prep guide for Analytical Thinking (AT) interviews. AT interviews assess: understanding a product in its company/market context, establishing metrics, identifying team goals, evaluating tradeoffs.

**Linear flow** (35 minutes of working time):
1. **Assumptions and game plan** (1 min): 3–4 assumptions to narrow scope; outline game plan so interviewer can redirect
2. **Product rationale**: Articulate product context (what it does, maturity, business model), market positioning (competitive landscape, differentiators), company/product alignment (mission statement)
3. **Metric framework**: Identify ecosystem players and their value propositions; define trackable metrics (specific enough for a data scientist to query); define a North Star Metric (NSM) with a time frame; critique the NSM with 1–2 guardrail metrics. Warning against averages/ratios as NSM. Spotify example: NSM = "total streaming hours per week."
4. **Goal-setting**: Altitude shift from product-level metrics to specific team-level goals

Examples used throughout: Spotify, Instagram Reels, DoorDash.[^1]
