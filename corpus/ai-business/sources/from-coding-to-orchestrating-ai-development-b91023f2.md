# From Coding to Orchestrating: Embracing AI in Development — Arvid Kahl

Source: thebootstrappedfounder.com. Ingested to [/ai-business/bootstrapped-saas-playbook.md].

AI assistance revealed that the author never loved coding — he loved what coding produced. Podscan evolved from 100% hand-coded, to AI-assisted, to fully agentic in 6 months. Developer-entrepreneur divide: pure developers enjoy the puzzle; entrepreneurs enjoy the business impact. AI separates those two things. "It turns out it's not that I love coding. What I actually love is getting code written and getting code deployed." Delegation reality: eventually you hire and others write code differently; AI just brings that moment earlier for $20/month.[^1]
