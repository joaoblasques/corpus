# Forget 1% Improvements: Focus on Customer Conversations — Arvid Kahl

Source: thebootstrappedfounder.com. Ingested to [/ai-business/bootstrapped-saas-playbook.md].

The "1% better every day" mantra leads to busywork (button color changes, headline tweaks) — unmeasurable and irrelevant. One conversation revealing a six-month-old wrong assumption, fixed, can jump retention 10%. Alternative: one conversation with a customer or prospect every day. After 365 you'll know the exact language customers use, their explicit and implicit needs, and spot pivotal insights. The 1% improvement to endorse: "Stay in touch with who you're doing this for every day."[^1]
