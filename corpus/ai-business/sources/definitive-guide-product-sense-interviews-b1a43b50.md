# The Definitive Guide to Mastering Product Sense Interviews — Ben Erez

Source: Lenny's Newsletter, by Ben Erez. Ingested to [/ai-business/technical-career.md].

Long-form guide for PM candidates on acing Product Sense (PS) interviews at top tech companies.

**Five evaluation dimensions** (candidates need passing score on each):
1. Clear communication: waypointing (pause-think-share-check-in), assumption setting (2–4 narrowing scope without closing doors), game plan articulation
2. Product motivation: product + value, strategic/competitive context, mission statement
3. Segmentation: ecosystem players, segment by behaviors/motivations (not just demographics), mutual exclusivity test, prioritize by reach × underserved degree, vivid persona
4. Problem identification: day-in-the-life user journey mapping (not generic pre/during/post), 3 pain points, prioritize by frequency × severity, connect to mission
5. Solution development: 3 meaningfully different solutions, impact vs. effort 2×2, v1 implementation + go-to-market, risks and mitigations, connect to mission

Three example questions: "Tell me about a product you love and how you'd improve it" (Claude Projects); "Design a product for gardening" (plant match AI for urban gardeners); "Build a podcast product for Netflix" (documentary audio extensions).[^1]
