# How to Think Like a Genius (The Map of All Knowledge) — Dan Koe

Source: thedankoe.com. Ingested to [/ai-business/creator-economy-philosophy.md].

Genius-level thinking is about clarity, not book smarts. The AQAL model (Ken Wilber) maps all knowledge into four quadrants: Individual Interior (thoughts/emotions/consciousness), Individual Exterior (behaviors/actions), Collective Interior (shared culture/values), Collective Exterior (systems/structures/institutions). Most people only think from one quadrant. Four levels of development: Premodern (authority/tradition), Modern (science/achievement), Postmodern (relativism), Second-Tier (synthesis — able to pull truths from all prior perspectives). "This is why smart people are incredibly dumb — they can't hold multiple perspectives."[^1]
