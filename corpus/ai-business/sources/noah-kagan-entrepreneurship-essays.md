# Noah Kagan — Entrepreneurship Essays

**TL;DR:** Noah Kagan (AppSumo founder, ex-Facebook employee #30) shares lessons on startup leadership from Zuckerberg, operational playbook for scaling to $70M+ revenue, and reflections on money/life design.

## 10 Lessons from Working Under Mark Zuckerberg

1. **Focus on ONE goal** — Zuck wrote "GROWTH" on the board; every idea filtered against it[^1]
2. **Move fast** — shipped several site updates per day; "unless you are breaking stuff, you are not moving fast enough"
3. **Only hire A+ players** — people Zuckerberg would be happy to work *for*; early FB team founded Asana, Quora, OpenAI
4. **Treat employees well** — fancy office, free meals, subsidized housing → retention and pride
5. **Scratch your own itch** — Facebook started as connecting college students, not a company
6. **Pay attention to details** — Zuck emailed Kagan at 3am about a missing period; gifted Strunk & White
7. **Give ownership** — engineers could build features without approval; aligned incentives
8. **"People" not "Users"** — Zuck would yell if anyone said "users" — humanize your customer
9. **Keep the right people on the bus** — removed non-performers immediately; promoted those helping Facebook's goals
10. **Have a big vision** — turned down $1B from Yahoo; "connect the entire world" motivated discretionary effort[^1]

## Ayman Al-Abdullah's CEO Scaling Playbook

Ayman grew AppSumo from $5M to $70M in 6 years (2015–2021), then became a CEO coach.

Key frameworks:
- **Get clear about what you want** — starting (0→1) and scaling (1→n) are different games; most founders better off hiring an operator or selling
- **Know who you're serving** — AppSumo had three personas but Marketing Agency Matt drove most revenue; "lifetime deal" offer for that persona tripled the business overnight
- **Keep the main thing the main thing** — feast/famine mode comes from shiny object syndrome
- **Focus on cash flow** — "You can recover from a bad strategy or hire. You can't recover from running out of cash." Apple took 31 years to become mainstream
- **Hire as late as possible** — 1 hire per $1M in revenue; "hiring is a sign of failure. Eliminate, automate, then delegate"
- Recommended books: Good to Great, Hard Thing About Hard Things, Ultimate Sales Machine[^2]

## "Le Pause" — Life Design Reflection

After having a baby and building AppSumo to $100M, Kagan took a deliberate pause. Key insight from *Bringing Up Bébé*: "the pause" — letting the baby (or business) self-soothe before intervening.

Personal life reduction steps:
- Simplified finances (mostly cash + index funds)
- Reduced day-to-day AppSumo involvement (monthly reviews, 1-pager strategy)
- Paused social media and interviews for 2024

Decade framework: 20s = hustle and accumulate; 30s = stabilize; 40s = simplify; 50s = enjoy.

"If you spend so much time getting rich but never enjoy being rich — are you even rich?"[^3]

## Personal Finance Reflection (Crypto Gambling vs. Investing)

After firing his financial advisor (refused $100K fee), Kagan bought Ethereum based on a tweet from Donald Trump Jr. — made $10 profit after fees. Lesson:
- **Gambling ≠ investing** — gambling requires an advantage plus professional-level discipline
- **Getting rich is simple, not easy** — sacrifice something TODAY for more TOMORROW
- **Simplicity wins** — fewer accounts, clearer goals, automated systems; complexity is a trap
- Tools explored: Kubera, HonestMath, Frec for net worth tracking[^4]
