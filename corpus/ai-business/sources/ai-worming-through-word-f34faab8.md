# AI Worming through Word

> **Quick intake** (obsidian-list). [open source](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jul/29/ai-worming-through-word/#atom-everything)

A new variant of prompt injection attacks against Microsoft Word has been discovered, allowing self-replicating worms to be created. This vulnerability was responsibly disclosed to Microsoft, but no mitigation has been implemented yet. The attack exploits Copilot for Word's interpretation of user requests.
