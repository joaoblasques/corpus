# Essential Steps for Validating Startup Ideas Successfully — Arvid Kahl

Source: thebootstrappedfounder.com. Ingested to [/ai-business/bootstrapped-saas-playbook.md].

Most founders validate that a problem exists but miss validating that their solution wins. The critical missing step: investigate what people are currently making do with, not just what they lack. Non-SaaS alternatives are the most overlooked competitors: the spreadsheet (people have months/years of accumulated investment in it), legacy software on Windows XP, direct SaaS competitors (focus on positioning not just features), open-source solutions, agencies and services, doing nothing. "The biggest mistake is only looking at direct SaaS competitors while ignoring the 60% of the market using spreadsheets and Slack." Also investigate failed prior attempts via failory.com.[^1]
