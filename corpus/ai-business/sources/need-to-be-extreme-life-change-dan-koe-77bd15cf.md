# You Need to Be Extreme If You Want Your Life to Change — Dan Koe

Source: thedankoe.com. Ingested to [/ai-business/creator-economy-philosophy.md].

Neuroplasticity (Hebb's Law: neurons that fire together, wire together) + novelty and challenge accelerate rewiring. Intensity and obsession stack neurochemicals: curiosity (dopamine/norepinephrine), passion, purpose (serotonin), autonomy (reduced cortisol), mastery (sustainable dopamine). Defense (remove distractions, automatic "no" to invitations) + Offense (four focus habits: one project, one book, one meditation, one workout). Ilya Prigogine's dissipative structures: when a system is pushed far from equilibrium, it can spontaneously emerge into new complex ordered patterns — order from chaos.[^1]
