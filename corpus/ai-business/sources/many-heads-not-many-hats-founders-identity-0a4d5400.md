# Many Heads, Not Many Hats: The Founder's Identity Crisis — Arvid Kahl

Source: thebootstrappedfounder.com. Ingested to [/ai-business/bootstrapped-saas-playbook.md].

Transitioning from consulting/agency to bootstrapped SaaS requires growing entirely new cognitive frameworks, not just switching roles. Consulting-to-SaaS mismatches: acquisition must be always-running (not just on churn), cold outreach replaces relationship-based sales, quantity with targeted quality replaces quality over quantity. Geoffrey Moore's Crossing the Chasm: early adopters don't want references and 5-year track records — they want excitement about new things. The cognitive dissonance to hold: believe strongly enough in your idea to ship it, AND assume your prior instincts might be actively wrong for this context.[^1]
