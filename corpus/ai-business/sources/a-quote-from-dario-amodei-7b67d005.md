# A quote from Dario Amodei

> **Quick intake** (obsidian-list). [open source](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Aug/16/dario-amodei/)

Dario Amodei discusses the public's negative view of AI, attributing it to a crisis of trust rather than warnings from AI leaders. He emphasizes the need for AI companies to deliver on their promises to benefit the world. Amodei also critiques AI companies for focusing on messaging and marketing rather than actual achievements.
