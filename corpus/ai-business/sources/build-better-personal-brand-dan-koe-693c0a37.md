# How to Build a Better Personal Brand Than 99% of People — Dan Koe

Source: thedankoe.com. Ingested to [/ai-business/creator-economy-philosophy.md].

"A personal brand isn't a business model. It's a traffic source. It's a trust mechanism." Trust Matrix: Growth (pattern recognition in what works — study anomalies with ≥2x usual engagement), Authenticity (time under attention, alignment of values, authentic polarization), Authority (teach through a new lens, persuade the non-interested). First monetization: product should take no longer than a week to build; charge ~$10; if conversion rate is over 2.5%, expand. AI prompt for analyzing successful posts: "Do a comprehensive analysis on this social post... what psychological tactics are being used, and how I can replicate this style step-by-step with my own ideas."[^1]
