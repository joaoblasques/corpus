# Future of Software: Building on Human-Generated Data — Arvid Kahl

Source: thebootstrappedfounder.com. Ingested to [/ai-business/bootstrapped-saas-playbook.md].

As software building costs fall to near zero, transformative SaaS is vulnerable — agents can replicate input→output transformations. The durable moat: human-generated data (podcasts, blog posts, genuine social content) that only its creator had the context to produce. Having data = half the moat; making data accessible (API, MCP, webhooks) = the other half. API-first principle: UI/REST API/MCP parity. Even behavioral metadata from platform usage constitutes unique data. Podscan's 50M transcribed podcast episodes cost tens of thousands of dollars/day for an agent to replicate.[^1]
