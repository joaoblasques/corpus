# How to Become the Main Character of Your Life — Dan Koe

Source: thedankoe.com. Ingested to [/ai-business/creator-economy-philosophy.md].

Three reasons for NPC behavior: conditioning (environmental programming), industrialization (school → job → retire system), first-tier thinking (>95% of Western population can't hold multiple perspectives). Three ingredients for agency: Awareness (widen aperture, spot opportunities), Access (exploit new technology), Agency (belief that all problems are soluble — Seligman's learned helplessness experiment shows two-thirds of the western population has learned helplessness). Agency is a habit trained through the experimental loop: Goal → Experiment → Experience → Confirmation. "Agency — and the good life — is belief in the difficult."[^1]
