# HUMAN 3.0 – A Map To Reach The Top 1% — Dan Koe

Source: thedankoe.com, 2026. Ingested to [/ai-business/creator-economy-philosophy.md].

Foundational introduction to Dan Koe's HUMAN 3.0 framework. Synthesizes psychology, philosophy, personal development, spirituality, technology, and business into one integrated model.

Five structural components: (1) Four quadrants (Mind/Body/Spirit/Vocation) adapted from Ken Wilber's AQAL; (2) Three macro levels (Conformist → Individualist → Synthesist) adapted from Spiral Dynamics; (3) Phases of transition (Dissonance → Uncertainty → Discovery); (4) Traits (horizontal development — knowledge, experience, skill — gating vertical level transitions); (5) Channels (exciting "quests" that appear at Dissonance phase).

Warning on False Transformation: imitating a higher level without required trait development. "Glitches" can accelerate entry: psychedelics, AI (described as "the most widely available Glitch that crosses into all domains — can be used to self-develop or self-destruct rapidly").

Archetypes and Metatypes: Archetypes are patterns at specific quadrant/level combinations; Metatypes are syntheses across all four archetypes with a scoring system (1–3 points per level, divided by 12).[^1]
