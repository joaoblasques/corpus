# The Death of Thoughtful Creation — Dan Koe

Source: thedankoe.com. Ingested to [/ai-business/creator-economy-philosophy.md].

"We're experiencing the death of thoughtful creation. The world is being filled with more factories rather than gardeners." You can't compete with machines where speed matters — that leaves humans with domains of meaning, play, and signal. Before industrialization, ~80% of free American workers were self-employed farmers or artisans; today only ~10% are self-employed. The solution: become a modern digital artisan. Taste is the competitive moat: "When machines can think about everything all at once, our job is to have standards." Six-month commitment pattern: one hour a day of pure craftsmanship.[^1]
