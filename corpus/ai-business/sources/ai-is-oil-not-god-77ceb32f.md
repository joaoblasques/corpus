# "AI is Oil, Not God"

> **Quick intake** (obsidian-list). [open source](https://www.notboring.co/p/ai-is-oil-not-god)

The author argues that AI is like oil, a valuable resource that can be harnessed and scaled, rather than a god-like entity that needs to be controlled. They support open-weight models, citing the benefits of commoditization and competition. The author acknowledges potential risks but believes they can be mitigated.

**Key topics**
- AI as oil
- Open-weight models
- Commoditization
- Competition
- Risk mitigation
- Regulation
