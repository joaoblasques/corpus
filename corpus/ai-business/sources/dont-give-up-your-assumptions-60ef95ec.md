# Don't Give Up… Your Assumptions — Arvid Kahl

Source: thebootstrappedfounder.com. Ingested to [/ai-business/bootstrapped-saas-playbook.md].

"Don't give up" is dangerous advice at the wrong inflection point — doubling down when you should be experimenting. The solution is experimental persistence: run parallel experiments (one group sees the old experience, another sees new positioning/feature/interface). If both groups improve: external problem. If only one: you've identified the real lever. The landing page example: Pieter Levels makes millions with outdated-looking pages — the assumption that beauty converts better may itself be wrong. "Don't give up *your assumptions* — test them instead."[^1]
