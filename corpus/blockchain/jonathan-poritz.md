# Jonathan A. Poritz

Mathematician at Colorado State University–Pueblo, Department of Mathematics and Physics. Author of *Yet Another Introductory Number Theory Textbook* (2014), an open-access undergraduate text covering divisibility, modular arithmetic, congruences, and RSA cryptology, used for Math 319.[^1]
