# Paul Volk

**TL;DR**: Marketing Technology Solution Architect at Databricks sitting "at the intersection of marketing strategy and data engineering" — leads the design of Databricks' own MarTech/CDP stack and its journey to a fully agentic, Lakehouse-native Customer Data Platform.[^src1]

## Role and focus

Paul Volk is a Marketing Technology Solution Architect at Databricks. He leads the design and evolution of Databricks' MarTech stack, guiding the team's journey from siloed channel execution to a fully agentic, Lakehouse-native CDP. He represents a practitioner angle: what it actually takes to operationalize data-driven marketing at scale on the Databricks platform.[^src1]

## Key themes

- Lakehouse-native Customer Data Platform (CustomerLake)
- Agentic marketing workflows powered by data engineering
- Closing the gap between what data teams build and what marketers can run

Presented at Data+AI Summit 2026: "Databricks on Databricks: Marketing with CustomerLake, the new Agentic CDP."[^src1]
