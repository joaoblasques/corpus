# Source: Junior Data Engineers Build Pipelines. Seniors Build Trust

**Authors**: Sahar Massachi (ex-Facebook DE+SWE), Zach Wilson (dataexpert.io)
**URL**: https://blog.dataexpert.io/p/data-engineering-was-never-about
**Series**: Part 3 of "The Data Warehouse Setup No One Taught You"
**Collected**: 2026-07-01

## Summary

Argues that by 2026, DE's job is fundamentally about being a **product owner for data** — not building pipelines. LLMs write SQL faster than most DEs; the durable value is figuring out what data products coworkers need, building them, and making them trusted and used. Trust is the key deliverable.

## Key claims

- "In 2026, Data Engineering is no longer about pipelines."[^src]
- AI makes engineering grunt work easier, which makes everything else (engineering strategy, people/product work) more important, not less.[^src]
- DE's job: figure out what data product will find market fit with coworkers (customers), then build, market, and showboat it.[^src]
- "If the team doesn't use your stuff — that's your fault, not theirs."[^src]
- Trust prerequisite: they must trust your priorities first (obsess about the surface they obsess about), then trust you as a person (low-latency, available, responsive), then they will trust your data.[^src]
- Showmanship story: Facebook DE who renamed a table and wrote an internal blog post → company-wide viral hit used for years.[^src]
- Moriarty model (the most successful DE Sahar knew): never let a question go unanswered for more than a few minutes; prioritized maximum availability and low-latency responses to key team members over deep focus time.[^src]
- Credibility is built to be spent: on better metrics, better architecture, buy-in for real infrastructure work.[^src]
