# Prashant Upadhyay

**TL;DR**: Sr. Solutions Architect at Databricks specializing in unifying operational and analytical data for real-time, AI-driven applications — streaming pipelines, OLTP/Lakehouse, and generative AI integration.[^src1]

## Role and focus

Prashant Upadhyay is a Sr. Solutions Architect at Databricks helping enterprises architect streaming pipelines, OLTP, and Lakehouse solutions. His focus is "turning high volume data into governed, production-ready intelligence" and bridging operational data platforms with generative AI and agentic systems.[^src1]

## Key expertise

- Streaming pipeline architecture on Databricks
- Lakehouse pattern for unified operational + analytical data
- Generative AI and agentic system integration on a single unified platform
