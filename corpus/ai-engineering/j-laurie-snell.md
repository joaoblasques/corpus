# J. Laurie Snell

Probabilist at Dartmouth College. Co-author with Charles M. Grinstead of *Introduction to Probability* (American Mathematical Society, 2003), released under the GNU Free Documentation License [^p01]. Originator of the CHANCE project, which uses probability and statistics to analyze current events in the news as a pedagogical tool. The textbook's simulation-first approach reflects Snell's emphasis on building intuition through computation before formal proofs.
