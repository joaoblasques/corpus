# AI Brain Fry: The Cognitive Overload of AI Development

**TL;DR**: Harvard Business Review (HBR) named a real phenomenon — "AI brain fry" — defined as mental fatigue from excessive use or oversight of AI tools beyond one's cognitive capacity. Nine concrete impacts on engineering work identified; practical mitigations proposed.

## The phenomenon

HBR definition (cited in[^src]):
> "We found that the phenomenon described in these posts—cognitive exhaustion from intensive oversight of AI agents—is both real and significant. We call it 'AI brain fry,' which we define as mental fatigue from excessive use or oversight of AI tools beyond one's cognitive capacity. Participants described a 'buzzing' feeling or a mental fog with difficulty focusing, slower decision-making, and headaches."

Costs: increased employee errors, decision fatigue, intention to quit.[^src]

## Nine impacts on engineering work

1. Senior+ engineers become glorified code reviewers
2. Impossible to keep pace with AI output volume in a codebase
3. Loss of understanding of the minutiae of AI-generated code
4. Mind/soul torn between deep review (right thing) and meeting expectations (fast thing)
5. Inexperienced devs and non-engineering stakeholders overestimate their competence (armed with Claude)
6. Bad designs and architecture are amplified (AI codifies them)
7. Faster burnout
8. "Letting things slide" that would normally be caught
9. More organizational and professional chaos and uncertainty[^src]

"The mental burden of the expectation that we use AI to move quickly and produce more, while still being held accountable for all negative outcomes and side effects" — a classic "responsible for what you can't fully control" problem.[^src]

## Mitigations

- Find hobbies, take time away from the computer; exercise; read non-technical material
- Treat AI coding as one skill among many in the marketplace, not the defining one
- Slow down on large design decisions — spend extra time on the AI-generated PR even if it appeared "instantly"
- Go deep on problem understanding; ship at pace when needed, slow down when it counts
- Ignore both AI Doomers and AI Groomers; take the middle road[^src]
