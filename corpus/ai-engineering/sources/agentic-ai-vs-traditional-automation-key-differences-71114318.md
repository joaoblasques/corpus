# "Agentic AI vs. Traditional Automation: Key Differences"

> **Quick intake** (obsidian-list). [open source](https://zencoder.ai/blog/agentic-ai-vs-traditional-automation)

Agentic AI is a type of AI system that can operate autonomously, perceive its environment, reason about goals, and take action on behalf of users. It goes beyond traditional automation by understanding objectives, creating plans, making decisions, adapting to changing conditions, and executing multi-step workflows with minimal human intervention. A hybrid approach combining agentic AI and automation can deliver the best business outcomes.

- Autonomous AI
