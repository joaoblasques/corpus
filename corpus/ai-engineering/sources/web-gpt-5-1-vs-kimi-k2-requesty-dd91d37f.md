# GPT-5.1 vs Kimi K2 — Requesty Benchmark Comparison

Source: https://www.requesty.ai/models/compare/openai--gpt-5.1/vertex--kimi-k2 (collected 2026-06-30)

- GPT-5.1 outperforms Kimi K2 on 7 of 10 benchmarks; Kimi K2 leads on math: AIME 2025 94.7% vs 94.0%, and τ²-Bench 93.0% vs 81.9%.
- Kimi K2 is cheaper: $0.60/$2.50 vs $1.25/$10.00 per 1M; 262K context but 262K max output (vs GPT-5.1's 128K).
- Kimi K2 scores: GPQA Diamond 83.8%, LiveCodeBench 85.3%, HLE 22.3%.
