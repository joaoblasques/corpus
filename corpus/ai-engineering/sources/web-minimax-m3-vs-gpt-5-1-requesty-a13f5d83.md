# MiniMax M3 vs GPT-5.1 — Requesty Benchmark Comparison

Source: https://www.requesty.ai/models/compare/minimaxi--minimax-m3/openai--gpt-5.1 (collected 2026-06-30)

- MiniMax M3 outperforms GPT-5.1 on 5 of 6 shared benchmarks: Intelligence Index 44.4%, GPQA Diamond 92.9%, τ²-Bench 88.9%, Coding Index 58.6%, HLE 37.1%.
- Priced at $0.30/$1.20 vs $1.25/$10.00 per 1M — roughly 4× cheaper input, 8× cheaper output; 1M context, 128K max output, reasoning supported.
- GPT-5.1 wins on Terminal-Bench Hard (45.5% vs 42.4%); math scores not reported for MiniMax M3.
