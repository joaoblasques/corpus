# "If I Started AI Freelancing in 2026, I'd Do This"

> **Source** (YouTube · Dave Ebbelaar · playlist _AI Freelancing_ · 2026-05-04). [watch on YouTube](https://youtu.be/CRYea6gFWgk) · [transcript](../../../raw/youtube/youtube-CRYea6gFWgk-if-i-started-ai-freelancing-in-2026-i-d-do-this.md)

## TL;DR

Dave Ebbelaar — six years full-time freelance AI engineer — condenses his experience into a three-level framework called the **Data Freelancer Blueprint**: (1) Get Going, (2) Get Paid, (3) Scale. The central argument: the barrier is psychological, not technical; what separates earners from experimenters is end-to-end delivery, not flashy AI.

---

## Level 1 — Get Going

The primary blocker is psychological, not technical.[^1] Most engineers believe they need years of experience, seniority, or certifications before charging clients — Ebbelaar argues none of that is required.

Real-world AI automation is described as "taking the boring and time-consuming processes in a business and simply making it less boring and time-consuming."[^1] The dominant category of work: moving data between systems, writing reports, doing analysis — processes that underpin "a large chunk of the global economy."[^1]

**Starting move**: build three projects in the "boring yet useful zone."[^1] Requirements for each:

- **End-to-end**: not just a local script but something deployed and integrated with a real system.[^1]
- **Low impressiveness bar**: the goal is demonstrating the ability to take an idea from concept to value delivery, not to showcase advanced ML.[^1]

The differentiator is deployment and integration, not code generation: "anyone nowadays can follow a YouTube tutorial or literally ask something to Claude Code and it will build you a script."[^1] What separates earners is "knowing how to take that code, integrate it with real systems and then deploy it somewhere so it becomes an actual working product."[^1]

Niche selection at this stage is explicitly deprioritized — "the tech industry is so complex that every project is going to be different."[^1]

**LinkedIn**: the only required asset at level 1. Minimum: updated bio, headline reflecting problems you solve, tech stack listed. Goal: "for someone in 10 seconds reviewing your profile to get a good idea of what you bring to the table."[^1]
