# Nano Banana 2 Lite — Gemini 3.1 Flash Lite Image (Simon Willison, 2026-06-30)

**Source**: simonwillison.net — `raw/_inbox/web-nano-banana-2-lite-96ca38fd.md`

## Summary

[Simon Willison](/ai-engineering/simon-willison.md) briefly noted the release of Google's Gemini 3.1 Flash Lite Image model, also internally known as "Nano Banana 2 Lite." [^1]

**API ID**: `gemini-3.1-flash-lite-image`

**Positioning**: Google's "fastest and cheapest Gemini image model, engineered for velocity and scale." [^1]

**Willison's test**: Used AI Studio to generate a "where's Waldo"-style image featuring a raccoon holding a ham radio. Judged the image generation quality better than earlier Nano Banana model tests from April. (Minor defect: misspelled "Forest Festival" in two different ways.) [^1]
