# GPT-5.1 vs GLM-5.1 — Requesty Benchmark Comparison

Source: https://www.requesty.ai/models/compare/openai--gpt-5.1/zai--glm-5.1 (collected 2026-06-30)

- GLM-5.1 (Z.ai) outperforms GPT-5.1 on 4 of 6 shared benchmarks: Intelligence Index 40.2%, τ²-Bench 97.7%, Coding Index 55.8%, HLE 28.0%.
- GLM-5.1 pricing: $1.40/$4.40 per 1M — slightly more expensive on input but cheaper on output vs GPT-5.1's $1.25/$10.00; 200K context vs 400K.
- GPT-5.1 leads on GPQA Diamond (87.3% vs 86.8%) and math benchmarks (GLM not reported).
