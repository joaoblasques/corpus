# GPT-4.1 vs GPT-5.1 — Requesty Benchmark Comparison

Source: https://www.requesty.ai/models/compare/openai--gpt-4.1/openai--gpt-5.1(collected 2026-06-30)

- GPT-5.1 outperforms GPT-4.1 on all 10 shared benchmarks; key scores: Math Index 94.0% vs 34.7%, GPQA Diamond 87.3% vs 66.6%, LiveCodeBench 86.8% vs 45.7%.
- Pricing: GPT-4.1 $2.00/$8.00 per 1M, GPT-5.1 $1.25/$10.00 per 1M; GPT-4.1 is cheaper on input, GPT-5.1 cheaper on output for typical I/O ratios.
- Context: GPT-4.1 has 1.0M context vs GPT-5.1's 400K; but GPT-5.1 has 128K max output vs GPT-4.1's 33K.
