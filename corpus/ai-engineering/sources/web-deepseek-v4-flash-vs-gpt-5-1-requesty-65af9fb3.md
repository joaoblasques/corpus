# DeepSeek V4 Flash vs GPT-5.1 — Requesty Benchmark Comparison

Source: https://www.requesty.ai/models/compare/deepseek--deepseek-v4-flash/openai--gpt-5.1 (collected 2026-06-30)

- DeepSeek V4 Flash outperforms GPT-5.1 on 5 of 6 shared benchmarks at ~10× cheaper input ($0.14 vs $1.25) and ~36× cheaper output ($0.28 vs $10.00).
- Key scores: Intelligence Index 40.3%, GPQA Diamond 89.4%, τ²-Bench 95.0%, SciCode 44.9%, HLE 32.1%.
- No vision, no reasoning mode; 1M context and 384K max output — both exceeding GPT-5.1.
