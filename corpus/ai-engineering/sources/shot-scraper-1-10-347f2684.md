# shot-scraper 1.10 Release (Simon Willison, June 2026)

**TL;DR**: shot-scraper 1.10 adds video demo recording via `shot-scraper video storyboard.yml`, enabling AI agents to record video demos of their own work.

Released June 30, 2026. shot-scraper is a CLI tool for:
- Screenshots of websites
- Recording video demos
- Scraping with JavaScript execution

Key new feature: `shot-scraper video storyboard.yml` — records video demos from a YAML storyboard. The release post notes the use case: "Have your agent record video demos of its work with shot-scraper video."
