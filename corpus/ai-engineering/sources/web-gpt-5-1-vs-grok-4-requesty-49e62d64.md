# GPT-5.1 vs Grok-4 — Requesty Benchmark Comparison

Source: https://www.requesty.ai/models/compare/openai--gpt-5.1/xai--grok-4 (collected 2026-06-30)

- GPT-5.1 leads on 8 of 10 benchmarks; Grok-4 wins on GPQA Diamond (87.7% vs 87.3%) and SciCode (45.7% vs 43.3%).
- Grok-4 is significantly more expensive: $3.00/$15.00 vs $1.25/$10.00 per 1M tokens; Grok-4 has computer use capability, GPT-5.1 does not.
- Grok-4 context: 256K; GPT-5.1: 400K.
