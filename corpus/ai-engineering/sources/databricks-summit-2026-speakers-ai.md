## Per-Speaker Notes

**Timothy Chen** — General Partner at Essence VC; "ex-CEO of Hyperpilot, Apache Foundation Member"; contributed to the Startup Forum meetup track. [^timothy]

**Callum Adamson** — "Co-founder and CEO of Applied Computing — we build foundation models for Energy Operations"; contributed to the Startup Forum meetup track. [^callum]

**Denny Lee** — Director of Developer Relations at Databricks; session: "Fireside Chat with Rohan Varma (OpenAI) and Ankit Mathur (Databricks) on Coding and Agents" under the Artificial Intelligence & Agents track. [^denny]

**Rohan Varma** — Product Manager at OpenAI; same fireside chat on coding and agents as Denny Lee and Ankit Mathur. [^rohan]

**Jai Behl** — Applied AI Architect at Anthropic; "I help enterprises put frontier AI to work … partner closely with customers to design, and scale solutions built on Claude. Before Anthropic, I spent 4.5 years at Databricks helping organizations … scale Data and AI, from GTM strategy to hands-on technical delivery." Session: autonomous data agents with Claude Managed Agents. [^jai]

**Xiaolei Shi** — Sr. Engineering Manager, Genie One at Databricks; session "Genie at Scale: Best Practices for Enterprise Rollout" in the Analytics & BI track. [^xiaolei]

**Daniel Gole** — "Data Scientist with a background in computational astrophysics" at Block; session "Building Query Expert MCP: Using RAG to Build an Analytics Agent That Goes Beyond Text-to-SQL" in the Artificial Intelligence & Agents track. [^daniel]
