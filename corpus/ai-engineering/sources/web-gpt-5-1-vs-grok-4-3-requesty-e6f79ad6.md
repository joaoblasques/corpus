# GPT-5.1 vs Grok-4.3 — Requesty Benchmark Comparison

Source: https://www.requesty.ai/models/compare/openai--gpt-5.1/xai--grok-4.3 (collected 2026-06-30)

- Grok-4.3 outperforms GPT-5.1 on 4 of 6 shared benchmarks: GPQA Diamond 90.1%, τ²-Bench 97.7%, SciCode 47.3%, HLE 35.0%.
- Grok-4.3 is cheaper on output: same $1.25 input but $2.50 output vs GPT-5.1's $10.00; also offers 1M context vs 400K.
- GPT-5.1 wins on Intelligence Index (38.9% vs 37.6%) and Terminal-Bench Hard (45.5% vs 37.9%).
