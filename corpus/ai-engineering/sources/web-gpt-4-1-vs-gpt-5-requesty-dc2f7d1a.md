# GPT-4.1 vs GPT-5 — Requesty Benchmark Comparison

Source: https://www.requesty.ai/models/compare/openai--gpt-4.1/openai--gpt-5 (collected 2026-06-30)

- GPT-5 outperforms GPT-4.1 on all 10 shared benchmarks; key scores: Math Index 94.3% vs 34.7%, GPQA Diamond 85.4% vs 66.6%, LiveCodeBench 84.6% vs 45.7%, HLE 26.5% vs 4.6%.
- Identical pricing to GPT-5.1 ($1.25/$10.00 per 1M); same 400K context and 128K max output.
- GPT-4.1 offers 1.0M context but no reasoning mode; GPT-5 has reasoning.
