# DeepSeek V4 Pro vs GPT-5.1 — Requesty Benchmark Comparison

Source: https://www.requesty.ai/models/compare/deepseek--deepseek-v4-pro/openai--gpt-5.1 (collected 2026-06-30)

- DeepSeek V4 Pro outperforms GPT-5.1 on all 6 shared benchmarks; top scores: τ²-Bench 96.2%, SciCode 50.0%, Intelligence Index 44.3%, GPQA Diamond 88.8%.
- Priced at $0.43/$0.87 per 1M vs GPT-5.1's $1.25/$10.00 — roughly 3× cheaper input, 11× cheaper output; 1M context vs 400K.
- No vision input and no reasoning mode; max output 384K tokens vs GPT-5.1's 128K.
