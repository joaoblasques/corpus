# Kimi K2

Moonshot AI's Kimi K2 model family. Strong math and agentic performance at mid-tier pricing.

**Kimi K2**: $0.60/$2.50 per 1M input/output tokens, 262K context, 262K max output. Vision, tool calling, reasoning, prompt caching. Leads GPT-5.1 on math: AIME 2025 94.7%, Math Index 94.7%. τ²-Bench 93.0%. GPT-5.1 leads overall (7 of 10 benchmarks).[^1]

**Kimi K2.7-code**: $0.95/$4.00 per 1M input/output tokens, 262K context, 262K max output. Code-focused variant: Intelligence Index 41.9%, Coding Index 60.8% (highest in this comparison set), GPQA Diamond 89.6%, τ²-Bench 90.1%, HLE 32.8%. Outperforms GPT-5.1 on 5 of 6 shared benchmarks.[^2]
