# StrongDM

**TL;DR**: StrongDM is a privileged access management (PAM) company that operates a 3-person AI Lab building security software using a "Software Factory" model — no human writes or reviews code. Notable as an early, high-profile case of the Dark Factory level of AI adoption.

## Company overview

StrongDM builds Universal Privileged Access Authorization (UPAA) — Zero Trust PAM for databases, servers, clusters, and cloud infrastructure. The AI Lab is a separate team within the company focused on building software entirely with coding agents.[^strongdm-blog]

## AI Lab

Founded July 2025. Initial team: Justin McCarthy, Jay Taylor, Navan Chauhan (3 people). The lab formed around an observed inflection point with Claude 3.5 Sonnet (October 2024 revision), after which long-horizon agentic coding began compounding correctness rather than errors.[^simonw]

Key principle: "no hand-coded software" — radical when they started, and now increasingly adopted across the industry as of early 2026.

The lab built a [Software Factory](/ai-engineering/software-factory.md) — including a Digital Twin Universe replicating Okta, Jira, Slack, Google Docs, Drive, and Sheets — using coding agents and API documentation, not manual coding.
