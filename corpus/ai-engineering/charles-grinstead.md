# Charles M. Grinstead

Mathematician at Swarthmore College. Co-author with J. Laurie Snell of *Introduction to Probability* (American Mathematical Society, 2003), a classic undergraduate probability textbook released under the GNU Free Documentation License via the CHANCE project [^p01]. The textbook is simulation-first: every distribution is introduced alongside a computer experiment before the formal definition.
