# Eugene Yan

**TL;DR**: Eugene Yan is an Applied Scientist at Amazon (based in Seattle, Singapore-origin) who writes extensively about ML systems in industry — LLMs, RecSys, RAG, evaluation, feature stores — and ML techniques (prompting, synthetic data). His site (eugeneyan.com) has 212 posts and 426,689 words; newsletter has 11,800+ subscribers.

## Background [^readme]

- **Role**: Applied Scientist at Amazon (works on LLM/ML systems)
- **Location**: Seattle, WA (originally from Singapore)
- **Work style**: strong preference for deep work in mornings; individual contributor over manager track; values fewer but stronger relationships; learning-focused, sharing-focused
- **Orientation**: pragmatic engineering view — writing covers what actually works in production ML, not just theory

## Focus Areas [^start]

**ML Systems in Industry**:
- LLMs and agents in production
- Recommender systems (RecSys) — long-standing expertise
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
- Evaluation methodologies (LLM-as-judge, behavioral evals)
- Feature stores and data infrastructure for ML

**ML Techniques**:
- Prompting strategies
- Synthetic data generation
- Evaluators (offline and online)
- Writing and communication in technical careers

## Writing and Research Output [^writing]

Active blogger from 2016 to present. Key topic clusters: evals, cybersecurity, AI productivity, leadership, LLMs, RecSys, personal reflections. Also writes on: how AI is affecting coding workflows, software quality under AI pair programming, eval design for production systems.

## Prototypes [^proto]

Notable projects (2016–2026):
- LLM recommendation systems
- Agentic workflows for research tasks
- RAG-based writing draft assistants
- AI coaches
- Evaluation frameworks for ML systems

## What He Helps With [^help]

Consulting and advisory in: ML/RecSys/LLM strategy, team structure/org design, industry introductions, speaking engagements. Declines: content collaboration, job-hunt Q&A, general bug debugging.
