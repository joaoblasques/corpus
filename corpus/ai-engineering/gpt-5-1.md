# GPT-5.1

OpenAI's frontier reasoning model as of mid-2026. Priced at $1.25/$10.00 per 1M input/output tokens with a 400K context window and 128K max output. Supports vision, tool calling, reasoning, and prompt caching.[^1]

Outperforms GPT-4.1 on all 10 benchmarks tested; leads most frontier models on Math Index (94.0%) and AIME 2025 (94.0%), and scores 86.8% on LiveCodeBench. Multiple cheaper models (DeepSeek V4, Qwen3.7-Plus, GLM-5.1) exceed it on τ²-Bench and Coding Index.[^1]
