# DeepSeek V4

DeepSeek's mid-2026 frontier model family. Exceptional price-to-performance ratio; both variants lack vision input and reasoning mode but outperform GPT-5.1 on most shared benchmarks, particularly τ²-Bench (tool use).

**DeepSeek V4 Pro**: $0.43/$0.87 per 1M input/output tokens, 1M context, 384K max output. Intelligence Index 44.3%, GPQA Diamond 88.8%, τ²-Bench 96.2%, SciCode 50.0%, HLE 35.9%. Outperforms GPT-5.1 on all 6 shared benchmarks.[^1]

**DeepSeek V4 Flash**: $0.14/$0.28 per 1M input/output tokens, 1M context, 384K max output. Intelligence Index 40.3%, GPQA Diamond 89.4%, τ²-Bench 95.0%, SciCode 44.9%, HLE 32.1%. Outperforms GPT-5.1 on 5 of 6 shared benchmarks at ~10× lower input cost.[^2]
