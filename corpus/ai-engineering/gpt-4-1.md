# GPT-4.1

OpenAI model with the largest context window in the OpenAI lineup at 1.0M tokens, but lower benchmark scores than GPT-5/5.1. Priced at $2.00/$8.00 per 1M input/output tokens; max output limited to 33K tokens. No reasoning mode, no computer use.[^1]

Key benchmark scores: Intelligence Index 19.4%, GPQA Diamond 66.6%, AIME 2025 34.7%, LiveCodeBench 45.7%, HLE 4.6%.[^1] Outperformed by GPT-5.1 on all 10 benchmarks tested.
