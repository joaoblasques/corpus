# Brendan Fong

MIT researcher in applied category theory; co-author (with David I. Spivak) of *Seven Sketches in Compositionality: An Invitation to Applied Category Theory* (arXiv:1803.05316, 2018). Works on the categorical foundations of machine learning, compositional game theory, and bidirectional programming (optics framework). See [/software-engineering/sources/seven-sketches-in-compositionality.md](/software-engineering/sources/seven-sketches-in-compositionality.md).
