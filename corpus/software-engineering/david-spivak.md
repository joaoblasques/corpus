# David I. Spivak

MIT researcher in applied category theory; co-author (with Brendan Fong) of *Seven Sketches in Compositionality* (arXiv:1803.05316, 2018). Known for formalizing database schemas as categories, operadic composition, and the *polynomial functor* framework for dynamical systems. See [/software-engineering/sources/seven-sketches-in-compositionality.md](/software-engineering/sources/seven-sketches-in-compositionality.md).
