# Robert S. Boyer

**TL;DR:** Computer scientist at SRI International; co-creator with [J Strother Moore](/software-engineering/j-strother-moore.md) of the Boyer-Moore automated theorem prover, documented in the 1979 ACM monograph *A Computational Logic*.

## Affiliation and work

Boyer was at SRI International, Menlo Park, California as of 1979. [^p01] His collaboration with Moore produced *A Computational Logic* (Academic Press, 1979), published in the ACM Monograph Series under editor Thomas A. Standish (UC Irvine). [^p01]

The book addresses automatic theorem proving — specifically, mechanically verifying program correctness through induction — structured across chapters covering the formal theory, proof heuristics, type information, rewrite rules, and worked examples such as a tautology checker. [^p01]

## Funding

Research was supported by NSF Grant MCS-7681425 and ONR Contract N00014-75-C-0816. [^p01]

## Note on later work

The Boyer-Moore theorem prover evolved into NQTHM and later ACL2. [unsourced — general knowledge]
