# Robert S. Boyer

Computer scientist at SRI International (Menlo Park, California) as of 1979. Co-author with [J Strother Moore](/software-engineering/j-strother-moore.md) of *A Computational Logic* (Academic Press, 1979) — the foundational text establishing the Boyer-Moore theorem prover, the first automated theorem prover capable of mechanically proving substantial program correctness results via induction. [^p01]

Work at SRI was supported by NSF Grant MCS-7681425 and ONR Contract N00014-75-C-0816. [^p01]

The Boyer-Moore theorem prover evolved into NQTHM and later contributed to ACL2 (A Computational Logic for Applicative Common Lisp), which remains in industrial use for hardware and software verification. [unsourced — general knowledge]

See [Formal Verification and Mechanical Theorem Proving](/software-engineering/formal-verification.md) and [A Computational Logic (source)](/software-engineering/sources/a-computational-logic-1979.md) for the technical content.
