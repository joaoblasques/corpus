# Richard Pawson

**TL;DR:** PhD researcher at Trinity College Dublin (Computer Science), creator of the "naked objects" approach to business systems design. Formerly a Research Fellow at Computer Sciences Corporation. Advisor to the Irish Department of Social and Family Affairs (DSFA) for the Naked Object Architecture. Co-authored the *Naked Objects* book (Wiley, 2002) with Robert Matthews. Presented at OOPSLA 2001 and XP2003.

## Key contributions

- **Naked objects concept (2001–2004):** Identified that the use-case controller pattern — enabled by MVC adoption — systematically produces behaviourally-thin domain objects, and proposed exposing domain objects directly to users via auto-generated generic UIs. See [Naked Objects](/software-engineering/naked-objects.md).[^p01]
- **DSFA advisory role (1999–2003):** Led early exploratory workshops at the Irish Department of Social and Family Affairs; advised on the design of the *Naked Object Architecture* (originally *Expressive Object Architecture*) that became the foundation for the Child Benefit Administration system, live November 2002.[^p01]
- **Seven design guidelines (Chapter 5 of thesis):** Developed a methodology for applying naked objects covering project selection, team pre-requisites, exploration/delivery phase structure, object-first (not use-case-first) analysis, code-first prototyping, incremental delivery, and executable acceptance testing.[^p03]
- **CarServ experiment (2003):** Co-designed a controlled comparative experiment with Dan Haywood to quantify development effort and agility differences between 4-layer and naked-objects implementations of the same application.[^p04]
