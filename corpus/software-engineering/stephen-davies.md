# Stephen Davies

CS professor at the University of Mary Washington (UMW), Department of Computer Science, Fredericksburg, VA. Author of *A Cool Brisk Walk Through Discrete Mathematics* (v2.2.2, CC BY-SA 4.0), a practitioner-oriented discrete math textbook designed for CS undergraduates.[^1]
